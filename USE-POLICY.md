# Use and permission policy

Argus is published so it can be read. It is not open source. This page says what you may do without asking, what needs permission, how to ask, and what will never be granted. The binding terms are in [LICENSE](LICENSE); this page explains how they are applied.

## What you may do without asking

| Activity | Allowed | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Read the repository on GitHub | Yes | None |
| Link to it, star it, discuss it in public | Yes | Link to this repository, not to a copy |
| Quote a short passage in an article, talk, review or paper | Yes | Under 200 words per passage, with the author's name and a link to this repository |
| Cite it in academic work | Yes | Use [CITATION.cff](CITATION.cff) |
| Open an issue challenging a decision or reporting a broken assumption | Yes | Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) |

## What needs written permission

Permission is asked for and granted case by case. Each grant names who, what, for how long and for what purpose, and can be withdrawn.

| Tier | Covers | Usual answer | Usual conditions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Study** | Cloning to read offline, opening the console prototype, running the test suite, running the compose stack on one machine for personal learning | Usually granted, usually free | Attribution kept, no redistribution, no production use |
| **Academic** | Use in a course, thesis, lab or paper, including reproducing figures and tables | Usually granted free of charge | Citation per CITATION.cff, a copy of the published work, no redistribution of the repository itself |
| **Evaluation** | An organisation assessing the design for its own cloud exit, including a time boxed pilot | Granted by agreement | Named organisation, fixed term, no derivative published, written outcome shared with the author |
| **Deployment** | Running any part of Argus in production, or building a platform from its design, runbooks, policies or GitOps model | Granted by commercial agreement only | Licence fee or engagement, attribution, no sublicensing |
| **Derivative** | Adapting the documents, console or stack into another product, template, course, book or service | Granted by commercial agreement only | Named derivative, revenue terms, attribution, author's approval of the published form |

## What will not be granted

- Republishing the repository or any substantial part of it under another name, on any site, including a fork presented as original work.
- Removing or changing the author's name, the copyright notice or the licence.
- Using any of the text, code or data to train, fine tune or evaluate a machine learning model, or including it in a dataset for that purpose.
- Scraping or mirroring the repository into a search, code intelligence or AI product.
- Selling, renting or lending access to it.
- Registering the name Argus, its mark or a confusingly similar one for a cloud, infrastructure or software product. See [TRADEMARK.md](TRADEMARK.md).

## How to ask

Email **adilmunawarx@gmail.com** or open a discussion on GitHub with the subject *Argus permission request* and these five lines:

1. **Who you are.** Name, organisation, country, and a link that confirms it.
2. **What you want to use.** The paths or sections, or "the whole repository".
3. **For what.** Study, academic, evaluation, deployment or derivative, in one paragraph.
4. **For how long.** A date or a term.
5. **Where it will be seen.** Internal only, a paper, a product, a public repository.

You will get an answer within ten working days. A grant is only valid in writing from the author. Silence, a star, a fork or a like is not permission.

## When a grant is withdrawn

The author may withdraw a grant with thirty days' notice, or immediately if its conditions are broken. On withdrawal every copy is deleted and the author told in writing. Work already published under an academic grant stays published.

## Earlier MIT copies

Copies obtained before 1 November 2026 under the MIT licence keep that licence for that version only. MIT requires the original copyright notice to stay in every copy. A copy with the notice removed or another name added is outside MIT as well as outside this policy, and is treated under [ENFORCEMENT.md](ENFORCEMENT.md).
